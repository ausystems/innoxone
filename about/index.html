About INNOXONE

A better choice. For better golf.

We’re a California golf mobility company. We design personal electric golf carts around the people who play.

Good design starts with people.

Young Kim’s work at INNODESIGN spans everyday electronics and the torch and cauldron for the PyeongChang 2018 Winter Olympics. Different products, with people at the center.

His lifelong interest in cars brought that thinking to golf. He asked a simple question: why should two golfers, playing two different balls, always share one ride?

The idea behind INNO-F1

Start with one golfer. Design around them.

A seat of your own. Your clubs close by. The freedom to go to your ball and prepare for your next shot.

INNO-F1 puts that idea into a single-rider electric golf cart. You still share the round, with your own way around the course.
