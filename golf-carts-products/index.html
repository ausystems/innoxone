Your ball. Your rhythm.

Golf brings people together. Every shot still belongs to you. INNO-F1 Original gives you a single-rider electric golf cart, so you can head to your own ball with your clubs close by.

The idea behind Original

It began with one golfer.

Young Kim and the INNOXONE team designed INNO-F1 from the ground up around one golfer, one golf bag and the freedom to move independently. That personal starting point shaped the whole idea.

Picture the moment after a tee shot. Your playing partner heads one way. Your ball is the other way. With a ride of your own, you can each find your next shot, choose where to stop and meet again as the round unfolds.

For your round

More attention for your next shot.

Your clubs, your route and your place to pause. Original lets you move to your own ball while staying part of the group. It is a small change in how you travel, with a very personal place in how you play.

A new family of personal golf mobility, born from a golfer’s wish to choose how the next nine holes feel. Coming soon at the 2027 Orlando PGA Show.

2027 collection preview

The story behind ROBOCADDY

Nine holes. One simple question.

Several years ago, Young Kim was walking Palo Alto Hills Golf & Country Club in California with a powered caddy carrying his bag. After nine holes, he felt tired. He wanted to ride the remaining nine.

That moment stayed with him. At the 2025 PGA Show, he asked golfers whether they would rather walk or ride. The more revealing question was whether they wanted the freedom to do both during the same round. That idea became the heart of ROBOCADDY.

Walk.Ride.Choose.

The next chapter of Walk & Ride Golf.

One family. More ways to play.

A choice for the way you move.

Meet the vision for F1, F2, SOLO and DUO. The collection will be introduced at the January 2027 PGA Show in Orlando.

F1
Personal single-rider mobility for golfers who want to ride independently.
F2
Intelligent two-person mobility, designed around walking, riding or a combination of both.
SOLO
A personal robotic golf companion envisioned to follow one walking golfer and carry their equipment.
DUO
An intelligent robotic caddy envisioned to support two walking golfers and their equipment.

Future product concepts. Ask our team about the reveal, specifications and availability.

For golfers

Your round can change as you do.

Enjoy the walk. Choose the ride. The ROBOCADDY vision is personal mobility that gives golfers more choice within the same round, with purpose-built designs for different ways to play.

For course operators

A broader way to think about mobility.

The longer-term vision pairs ROBOCADDY with INNOXONE CONNECT AI, a planned platform linking golfers, vehicles and course teams. Improvements to fleet use and course operations are goals to explore through course pilots.

Frequently asked questions

Choosing a golf cart is personal. Start here, or talk it through with our team.

Which INNO-F1 is right for me?

Original and Light are single-rider electric golf carts with dedicated golf-bag space. Light has a slim roof frame and an open-sided layout. Marshal is configured for course staff, with a rear utility box. Our team can help you choose.

How do I place an order?

Choose a model and select Request a quote in its product details, or call 949-384-XONE (9663). Our team will confirm your configuration, pricing, availability and delivery arrangements before you place an order.

Can I see a golf cart in person?

Contact us to arrange a showroom visit at 31896 Plaza Dr Suite D2, San Juan Capistrano, California. Ask which models are available to see and whether a demonstration can be arranged for your visit.

When will ROBOCADDY be revealed?

The ROBOCADDY collection is coming to the 2027 Orlando PGA Show in January 2027. The silhouettes are a collection preview. Contact our team with questions about the reveal and future availability.
