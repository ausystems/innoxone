The story of INNO-F1

Two golfers. Two different balls. Why one ride?

A different question. A different kind of golf cart.

Two golfers can share a round without taking the same route. Young Kim saw a chance to design an electric golf cart around one player, one golf bag and the next shot.

A designer’s eye. A golfer’s freedom.

In his PGA Show interview, Young Kim connects his experience designing everyday products with his lifelong interest in cars. For INNO-F1, he brought that thinking to a simple question: why should a golf cart always be built for two?

One golfer. The starting point for every detail.

INNO-F1 Original · Single-rider electric golf cart

Start with your own game.

Golfers hit different shots and head in different directions. A cart built for one lets you go straight to your own ball, without taking a shared cart to another player’s shot first.

Put the essentials around you.

INNO-F1 brings together a single seat, steering and controls within reach, and space for your golf bag. You drive the cart. Your clubs come with you.

Keep the round your own.

Choose where to stop, get ready for your next shot and enjoy your own space. You still share the round with your group, with the freedom to move independently.

From a new idea to your next round.

That is the idea behind INNO-F1: a personal electric golf cart that lets you follow your own ball. Sit in it, drive it and see how it fits the way you play.

Why ROBOCADDY was born

Golf has always been a game of movement.

For generations, golfers walked the fairways, experiencing the course one step at a time. Then golf carts made the game more accessible and convenient.

But golfers have always had to choose: to walk, to ride, or to compromise. We created ROBOCADDY to give golfers a better choice. Walk, ride, or do both, and make golf more enjoyable.

What is an INNO-F1 single-rider golf cart?

INNO-F1 is an electric golf cart built for one driver. Original and Light give golfers their own seat and space for a golf bag, so each player can drive to their own ball. Marshal provides rear utility storage for course teams. INNOXONE creates personal golf mobility for individual golfers, clubs, public courses and resorts.

Which INNO-F1 is right for me?

Original is designed for personal golf mobility. Light offers a simplified single-rider configuration. Marshal focuses on course operations and player assistance, with rear utility storage. Contact us to discuss the configurations currently available.
